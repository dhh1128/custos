# Companions — roadmap

Seven companions travel beside the ratified kernel. All are
informative: the kernel rules on any divergence, and nothing
here is normative for Custos or anyone else.

Two of the seven are named by role inside the ratified text, which
makes their absence a defect rather than a gap in a roadmap. The
engagement companion is now present. The **notation register** of
§14 is not, and its production is owed by an enactment rather than
by an edit — see the engagement companion's closing section.
